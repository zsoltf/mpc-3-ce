Launch Control XL3 guide

Control MPC tracks, drum pads, Q-Links and effects. The XL3 display follows MPC values, including changes made on the touchscreen.

Connect the XL3

  1. Connect the XL3 to the MPC by USB.
  2. In Preferences > MIDI/Sync, turn Track, Global and Control off for LCXL3 1 DAW In. Leave the Custom and musical ports configured as usual.
  3. Open Preferences > CONTROLLERS and press Detect. CE adds the XL3 and enters DAW Mixer mode.

The built-in integration provides Q-Link control and feedback without a Global MIDI Learn mapping.

Control Q-Links and effects

  1. On the XL3, press Mode, choose DAW Control, then press Mode again.
  2. Use the Page arrows to choose Q-Links 1–8 or Q-Links 9–16.
  3. Select the desired Q-Link mode and assignments on the MPC.
  4. Turn the top row of eight encoders to adjust those Q-Links. The display shows their numbers and current values.

The Page arrows cycle through six pages:

PageTop eight encoders
Q-Links 1–8The first eight Q-Links in the active MPC Q-Link mode.
Q-Links 9–16The next eight Q-Links in the active MPC Q-Link mode.
Effect slots 1–4One page per slot, showing its first eight available parameters in the current MPC context.

Choose the MPC Q-Link mode on the MPC; the XL3 Page arrows change control pages. In DAW Control, the middle and bottom encoder rows are unassigned. Faders retain the current Track or Pad mixer target.

Mix tracks

Press Mode, choose DAW Mixer, then press Mode again.

ControlAction
Eight fadersVolume for the current eight tracks.
Top encoder rowSend 1.
Middle encoder rowSend 2.
Bottom encoder rowPan.
Track arrowsPrevious or next bank of eight tracks.
Solo/ArmSwitch the upper button row between Solo and Record Arm.
Mute/SelectSwitch the lower button row between Mute and track selection.
PlayStart, pause in place, or resume playback.
RecordControl transport recording.

Faders use pickup: move a fader to the current MPC value before it takes control. Encoders act relatively. A control that is unavailable for the current track stays dark.

Mix drum pads

In DAW Mixer, hold Shift and press a Drum track’s lower-row button. This selects the track and opens its Pad mixer. Selecting a non-Drum track returns to Track mixing.

ControlPad mixer
FadersPad volume.
Bottom encodersPad pan.
Upper / lower buttonsPad Solo / Mute.
Track arrowsBank through all 128 pads, A01 to H16.
Shift + Solo/ArmSwitch between Track and Pad mixing for the selected Drum program.

The Pad mixer follows the selected Drum program. Pad sends, selection and record arm are unavailable. Q-Link and effect pages remain accessible in DAW Control.

Display and feedback

  • The display shows track or pad labels, Q-Link numbers and available effect parameter names. Values use a 0–127 scale and follow changes made on the MPC.
  • In Track mixing, knob lights use each track’s MPC colour. Pad mixing and DAW Control use fixed colours; knob LEDs indicate availability rather than a value ring.
  • Button lights follow selection, mute, solo and record-arm state. In Pad mixing, the two rows show Solo and Mute.
  • Hold Shift and turn a control to preview it. An unavailable encoder value shows a dash.
  • Disable the XL3 row in MPC Controllers before unplugging to return the device to Standalone mode.

For other controllers, see the controller reference.
