More control for your MPC.

Motorized faders, clip launching, a step sequencer and hands-on mixing for your MPC Live II. MPC 3 CE, the Controller Edition, is free firmware built in your browser from Akai's 3.9.1 update.

Clips on one Launchpad, drums on the other, and mixing on the XL3 and X-Touch.

New in v0.3.0.

  • Clip lightsClips show their track colour. The playing clip pulses white, queued clips flash and recording pulses red.
  • Drum steps64 steps for one sound or 16 for four, with a playhead and MPC Undo. Pick a clip to program it without launching.
  • Same on both LaunchpadsSession, Note and Custom work the same on X and Pro. Pan pads have an exact centre, lit white.
  • Track buttons on the ProThe row under the Launchpad Pro grid selects tracks, or arms, mutes, solos or stops them. Tap to keep a mode, hold for a moment.
  • Faders for your drum padsA Launch Control XL3 mixes tracks and drum pads, sends, effects and the MPC’s Q-Link knobs. Knobs show track colours; Shift + Select picks a track.
  • Live metersX-Touch meters keep running while you use other controllers, without flicker.
  • Patch synths on the touchscreenPd and Cardinal synth and effect patches you edit with your fingers, recalled with your project. Copy the plugin bundle to your SD card.

Set up in one press.

New in v0.3.0: Detect adds every connected X-Touch, X-Touch Mini, Launchpad and XL3.

  1. Set the controller's mode. Launchpads and the XL3 switch automatically.
  2. In Preferences > MIDI/Sync, turn Track, Global and Control off for its input.
  3. Plug it in, open Preferences > CONTROLLERS and press Detect. Existing rows stay as they are.

Known limits

  • Built on MPC 3.9.1; MPC 3.10 is not supported yet. Online Update replaces CE with stock MPC.
  • Cardinal MIDI and Terrorform play up to four voices. Demanding patches can still overload audio; avoid the Waves example.
  • Launchpad drum steps use a fixed colour palette, not MPC pad colours.
  • The XL3 pad mixer covers volume, pan, Solo and Mute.
  • Akai profiles need a saved program on the controller. Some MCU/HUI workflows are untested.
  • Tested on MPC Live II. Controller reconnects during playback and long sessions have limited testing.

Launch clips and draw drum steps.

Launch clips, scenes and Arrangement from a Launchpad X or Pro Mk3. Pads show your track colours and follow the MPC's clip launcher.

Session shows clips, Note edits drum steps and Custom opens the mixer. Hold Note and press a clip to program it without launching it. An empty slot gets a new clip.

Two Launchpads can work together, one on clips and one on drums.

Edit notes with a mouse.

Plug a USB mouse into the MPC. Click a note or drag a box around several, then roll the wheel: it's the MPC's data wheel.

Right-click and long-press gestures aren't supported yet.

Drag across a few notes to select them.

Step through notes with the wheel.

In the Grid, hold Shift and turn the data wheel to select the next or previous note in time order, like Maschine's event select. Let go of Shift and the wheel works as usual.

No controller needed. On the X-Touch, Shift and the jog wheel do the same.

Get it.

  1. Build it in your browser

    Open the image builder and choose Akai's official MPC-3.9.1-Gen1-update.img. The image is made on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

  2. Copy it to a USB drive

    Put the saved image at the top of the drive, on its own, with its filename unchanged.

  3. Update the MPC

    Back up your projects, connect the power adapter and run USB Drive Update from Preferences > Info > Update. Your settings and projects are kept. Download and unzip the matching SD plugin ZIP. With the MPC off, copy its MPCLEARN folder to the SD card root for Pd and Cardinal.
